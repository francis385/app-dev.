# 🎬 My Favorite Movie: Toy Story (1995)

## ⭐ About the Movie
Toy Story is my favorite animated movie. It is about toys that come to life when humans are not around. The story follows Woody, a cowboy doll, and Buzz Lightyear, a space ranger.

### 🧸 Main Characters
- **Woody** – A loyal cowboy doll.
- **Buzz Lightyear** – A space ranger who thinks he is a real hero.
- **Mr. Potato Head** – A funny toy.
- **Rex** – A nervous green dinosaur.

### ❤️ Why I Like This Movie
I like Toy Story because it is funny, exciting, and heartwarming. It teaches us about friendship, teamwork, and loyalty.

### 💬 My Favorite Quote
> "To infinity and beyond!"

### 🎥 Movie Information
- **Title:** Toy Story
- **Year Released:** 1995
- **Genre:** Animation, Adventure, Comedy
- **Director:** John Lasseter
- **Studio:** Pixar Animation Studios

**10/10 – A wonderful movie!**
